    Miam

    Miam is an open-source recipe app to write, save, and share recipes with the people you cook with. Self-hosted, mobile-friendly, and built around the way real kitchens work — from a screenshot on Instagram to a printable shopping list.

    What's inside?

    •   Write your own recipes with photos, tags, and steps
    •   Import from Instagram, scanned images (OCR) or manually
    •   Plan meals and build shopping lists in one tap
    •   Share privately with friends and family
    •   Export recipes to Word
    •   Own your data — self-hosted, Google sign-in
